# Whole-v3 Symbol Systems

## How to read a passport

`whole-v3` contains 32 systems and 671 coordinates. The selector gives every
system equal first-stage weight, then chooses a symbol inside that system. A
large catalogue therefore does not dominate a small one.

The passports below describe provenance, a possible distinction for attention,
and a guard against overclaiming. They are reference material after selection,
not selector input. **No system has a mandatory response role.** A coordinate
may become a function, verb, relation, rhythm, image, constraint, description,
or no usable difference in the current response.

## Archetypal and textual cycles

### `tarot` — Tarot (78)

**Provenance:** Classical 78-card Tarot vocabulary across Major and Minor Arcana.
**Attention:** Offers archetypal situations, transitions, relations, elemental suits, and movement through a cycle.
**Guard:** Do not treat a card as fate, prediction, diagnosis, or an instruction the user must obey.

### `iching` — I Ching (64)

**Provenance:** The Chinese Yijing hexagram cycle, represented as 64 named configurations.
**Attention:** Offers a geometry of change, tension between conditions, and the phase through which a situation may be moving.
**Guard:** Do not claim to perform traditional line divination when Altar has selected only a hexagram coordinate.

### `runes` — Runes (24)

**Provenance:** The 24 characters of the Elder Futhark with modern symbolic associations.
**Attention:** Offers compact forces, actions, exchanges, needs, thresholds, and forms of movement.
**Guard:** Do not present modern esoteric meanings as a complete historical account of Germanic runic practice.

### `hebrew-letters` — Hebrew Letters (22)

**Provenance:** The Hebrew alphabet carried with selected Western esoteric correspondences.
**Attention:** Offers a letter as generative form, sound, number, path, or structural distinction.
**Guard:** Distinguish living Hebrew language and Jewish traditions from later Western occult mappings; never claim lineage authority.

### `gene-keys` — Gene Keys (64)

**Provenance:** A contemporary contemplative system of 64 numbered keys aligned with the I Ching hexagram cycle; this pack carries only the numbered keys and their public hexagram correspondence.
**Attention:** Offers a numbered key whose hexagram correspondence may open change, tension, or phase; any deeper transformation gradient belongs to the source teaching, not to this coordinate.
**Guard:** Do not diagnose identity, and do not reproduce protected Gene Keys source teachings; the facets deliberately stop at the public hexagram correspondence.

## On-demand symbol facets

Default receipts intentionally omit descriptions. After the first unframed
meeting, `describe` can retrieve a symbol's compact facets, tradition, and
caution:

```bash
python scripts/altar.py describe --system elements --symbol 001-fire
```

Use those facets as optional associations. They may expand contact, but they do
not override the intelligence's own response delta and do not need to appear in
the outward answer.
