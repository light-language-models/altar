# Blinded playtest protocol

Separate four questions:

1. Does another runtime reproduce the same receipt?
2. Do independent blinded judges match question–symbol pairs above controls?
3. Does access to the field measurably change answer qualities?
4. Does adding a consenting observer or Matrix change those effects?

Pre-register timestamps, inclusion rules, primary metric, control construction,
and exclusions. Freeze the symbol pack and selector version. Keep questions out
of the selector. For correspondence, compare the true question–symbol pairing
against shuffled time-matched controls using judges blinded to condition. For
answer influence, compare responses generated with hidden field, placebo field,
and no field. Record `shifted`, `unclear`, and `no-shift` before seeing outcomes.

Report null results and uncertainty. Reproducibility of the hash mechanism is
not evidence of correspondence. Correspondence is not evidence of a quantum
mechanism. Observer influence needs its own randomized, blinded comparison and
must not be inferred from compelling anecdotes alone.
